Halloween Brownies

Description

Fudgy, thick, warm brownies. Perfect for fall weather and halloween parties.

Prep time: 15 minutes

Cook time: 25 to 30 minutes

Total time: 40 to 45 minutes

Servings: As many pieces you can cut!

Ingredients

  • 4 oz unsweetened baking chocolate,
  • 1/2 cup (1 stick) unsalted butter
  • 1 cup granulated sugar
  • 1/2 cup unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1/2 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • Decorations: Halloween M&Ms, Halloween sprinkles, candy corn, and edible candy eyeballs

Steps

  1. Preheat your oven to 350°F. Line an 8x8 inch baking pan with parchment paper
  2. Melt the unsweetened baking chocolate and butter together. Stir until smooth.
  3. Whisk in the sugar and cocoa powder until well combined.
  4. Add the eggs and vanilla extract and mix until fully mixed.
  5. Mix in the flour and salt just until no dry streaks remain. Do not overmix.
  6. Pour the batter into a pan and spread it evenly.
  7. Bake for 25 to 30 minutes.
  8. Let the brownies cool completely in the pan, then cut into squares.
  9. Decorate with Halloween M&Ms, sprinkles, candy corn, and edible candy eyeballs.

Halloween Monster Cookies

Description

Thick, soft, and chewy monster cookies packed with peanut butter, oats, chocolate chips, M&Ms, and sprinkles.

Prep time: 15 minutes

Cook time: 11 to 13 minutes

Total time: 26 to 28 minutes

Servings: ~16 cookies

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup unsalted butter, softened at room temperature
  • 1/2 cup creamy peanut butter
  • 3/4 cup brown sugar
  • 1/4 cup granulated cane sugar
  • 1 large whole egg
  • 1 large egg yolk
  • 1 1/4 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 cup old-fashioned oats
  • 1 tablespoon cornstarch
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 2/3 cup chocolate chips
  • 1/3cup white chocolate chips
  • 1 cup Halloween colored M&Ms
  • 3 tablespoons Halloween sprinkle mix
  • Sprinkle candy eyeballs

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 375°F. Line two large baking sheets with parchment paper.
  2. In a bowl, whisk together the flour, oats, cornstarch, baking soda, and salt. Set aside.
  3. In a large mixing bowl, use an electric mixer to cream the softened butter, brown sugar, and granulated sugar until light and fluffy.
  4. Add the egg and egg yolk and mix until combined. Add the peanut butter and vanilla extract, then mix on medium speed until fully incorporated.
  5. Add the dry ingredients to the wet ingredients and mix on low speed just until combined. Do not overmix.
  6. When just a few streaks of flour remain, fold in the semi-sweet chocolate chips, white chocolate chips, Halloween M&Ms, and Halloween sprinkles until evenly distributed.
  7. Scoop the dough with a 2-tablespoon cookie scoop (or large spoon) onto the prepared baking sheets, spacing them about 2 inches apart. Optionally add a few more sprinkles on top.
  8. Bake for 11 to 13 minutes. Immediately after removing from the oven, gently press 1 to 2 candy eyeballs onto each cookie
  9. Let it cool down

Halloween Feet Loaf

Description

A funny Halloween twist on meatloaf, shaped into two feet complete with onion toenails and ketchup. Perfect for a fun october dinner.

Prep time: 15 minutes

Cook time: 40 to 50 minutes

Total time: 1 hour

Servings: ~12

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs ground beef
  • 2 large eggs
  • 2 cups panko crumbs
  • 1 packet au jus
  • 1/2 tsp freshly cracked pepper
  • 1/2 tsp dry mustard
  • 1 tsp onion powder
  • 1 medium yellow onion
  • 1 cup ketchup

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 375°F. Line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
  2. In a large bowl, whisk together the eggs,pepper, dry mustard, and onion powder.
  3. Mix in the ground beef and panko crumbs just until combined. Do not overmix.
  4. Divide the mixture in half and place each half on the parchment-lined baking sheet.
  5. Form each half into a foot shape.
  6. Cut a thick slice of the onion and use one ring to cut toenail shapes for each toe. Place them on the toes.
  7. Slice two small onion rings and place them on the ankle part of each foot.
  8. Spoon a little ketchup over each onion ring and let some drip down the sides for a bloody effect.
  9. Bake for 40 to 50 minutes.
  10. Cool for 5 minutes before serving. Serve with extra ketchup, stewed tomatoes, or brown gravy if desired.
